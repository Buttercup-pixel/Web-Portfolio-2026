about

Hi, I’m Johanna, based in Stockholm. I’ve spent 13+ years doing makeup for fashion, beauty and music, and somewhere along the way I wanted to shape the whole picture. So now I do art direction too.

Every year I get a new obsession. It’s been baking, crochet, pole dancing, nail art. This year it’s chess.

Let’s talk over a game, or just a fika.
